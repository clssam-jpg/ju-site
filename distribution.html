A warehouse in every major port city

Sabah isn't a compact state. From Kota Kinabalu in the west to Tawau in the east is nearly 470 kilometres by road — longer than the drive from Singapore to Kuala Lumpur. Trying to serve the whole state from a single warehouse simply doesn't work for fast-moving consumer goods. The distances are too great, the lead times too long, and the stockouts too damaging to retail relationships.

That's why Jaya Uni'ang has built our network the way we have: one warehouse in each of Sabah's major port cities, so that every retailer, foodservice operator and institutional customer has a distribution point reachable within a single working day's drive. It's a model we've grown into over four decades — and one we continue to refine.

The story of our four warehouses

Kota Kinabalu · 1983 · Head office

Our original location, founded in January 1983 on Mile 5, Jalan Penampang. This is where Jaya Uni'ang began as a plastic packaging distributor, and it remains our head office today — serving the west coast urban market (Kota Kinabalu, Penampang, Papar, Kota Belud, Tuaran, Ranau) and coordinating company-wide operations.

Tawau · 1984

Our first expansion warehouse, opened just one year after the company's founding. Tawau's location on Sabah's south-east coast — and its role as a gateway to the agriculture-and-palm-oil hinterland — made it the obvious second base. Today it serves the south-east corner of the state, including Semporna and Kunak.

Sandakan · 1987

Sandakan is the historic “Sunny Town” of Sabah — long a centre of trade and a major east-coast port. Our warehouse at Mile 3 Jalan Utara serves the city and its interior reaches, including Kinabatangan and the Gomantong-to-Bilit corridor.

Lahad Datu · 1989

Lahad Datu completed our east-coast coverage. Our warehouse on Industrial Tengah Nipah serves the town and the surrounding palm-oil and agricultural belt — a region where reliable distribution means reliable supply to estate canteens, town shops and worker-township retailers.

You can't distribute FMCG in Sabah the way you distribute in the Klang Valley. The geography demands a different network — and we've built one that fits. — How we think about coverage, four decades in
What the network delivers

Six things a four-warehouse model makes possible

01

Statewide reach

Every major population centre in Sabah is within a single-day delivery radius of at least one warehouse.

02

Category depth

All four warehouses stock the full catalog — not just the fast movers. No customer gets a reduced range because of where they are.

03

Local teams

Each warehouse is staffed by Sabahans who know their region — the roads, the customers, the seasonal patterns, the local preferences.

04

Cross-warehouse stock support

If one branch runs short on a high-demand SKU, we rebalance from another. Customers don't feel the shortage.

05

Route flexibility

Standard weekly routes for most customers, faster cycles for high-volume accounts, special runs for promotional launches.

06

Logistics & warehousing

For select partners we also offer warehousing and logistics support, drawing on the group's decades of storage and fleet expertise.
