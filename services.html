Our services

More than moving boxes

Jaya Uni'ang offers sales, marketing, brand development and logistics — the full set of services a brand owner needs to grow in East Malaysia, and a retailer needs to keep shelves full.

Six services

What we actually do

Distribution is the core, but it isn't the whole job. These are the six services our principals and customers draw on.

Distribution & delivery

Our core business. Weekly routes from four warehouses, reaching every major population centre in Sabah within a single working day's drive.

Fixed weekly delivery days per outletFaster cycles for high-volume accountsSpecial runs for promotional launchesCross-warehouse stock rebalancing

Sales & market coverage

Named trade teams organised by channel — modern trade, general trade and HoReCa — because each one buys differently and restocks at a different cadence.

Dedicated account executives per channelRoute-level coverage planningOrder-taking and van-sales modelsNew-outlet acquisition across all regions

Trade marketing & merchandising

Promotions, in-store activations and shelf execution coordinated with the brand teams behind the products we carry.

Promotional calendar planningPlanogram and shelf-share executionPOS material delivery and installationSeasonal and festive campaign support

Warehousing & logistics

For select partners we offer warehousing and logistics support, drawing on the group's four decades of storage and fleet expertise.

Ambient storage across four locationsOwn fleet — no third-party dependencyStock holding for principals entering SabahConsolidated delivery for multi-brand orders

Brand development

We help brand owners build presence in East Malaysia — a market that behaves differently from the Peninsula and rewards local knowledge.

Market entry planning for SabahChannel and pricing strategy inputCompetitive and category feedbackLaunch coordination across four branches

Reporting & market insight

Transparent reporting is what turns a distributor into a partner. Principals see what we see.

Sell-through reporting by outletStock cover and ageing visibilityReturns and damages reconciliationOn-the-ground insight from local teams
Service commitments

Three things you can hold us to

ReservedOrders are allocated against physical stock at the depot before they're confirmed.
ReportedSell-through, returns and shelf-share back to principals monthly, by outlet.
