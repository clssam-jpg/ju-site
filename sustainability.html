Sustainability

Doing this properly, for a long time

A distributor that has operated in one state for four decades has an obvious interest in that state staying healthy. Here's where we are — and where we're honestly not there yet.

We distribute plastic packaging. We run a fleet of lorries across a state with sensitive ecosystems. We are not going to pretend that our business has no footprint — it does, and the honest position is to be specific about what's being done and what isn't yet.

What the group is doing

Our parent, Thong Guan Industries Bhd, was the first company in Asia Pacific to commercialise bio-based polyethylene stretch film, in partnership with Dow Chemical. The group also operates the first 33-layer nano-technology film line in Asia Pacific — a technology that matters environmentally because thinner, stronger film means less plastic per pallet wrapped.

The group holds ISO 9001 (quality), ISO 14001 (environmental management) and ISO 45001 (occupational health and safety) certifications, audited on a recurring cycle rather than claimed once.

What we do in Sabah

  • Local manufacturing shortens the chain. Packaging made by Uniang Plastic Industries in Inanam doesn't cross the South China Sea before reaching a Sabah customer.
  • Route consolidation. A retailer taking one mixed delivery instead of six single-supplier deliveries means fewer vehicle movements per shelf filled.
  • Stock rotation discipline. First-expiry-first-out picking across all four warehouses reduces write-offs — the cheapest waste to eliminate is the waste you never create.
  • Cross-warehouse rebalancing. Moving surplus stock between branches rather than letting it age out in one location.

Where we're not there yet

Fleet electrification is not realistic on Sabah's east-coast routes with today's charging infrastructure. Recyclate content in the packaging we distribute is improving but still principal-dependent. We would rather state this plainly than publish a target we can't evidence.

Our people and communities

Sustainability isn't only environmental. A distributor employing Sabahans across four towns has a responsibility to those towns: employee welfare programmes, training investment, internal promotion over external hiring, and quiet support for Sabahan community causes without publicity.

The privilege to operate in a state is earned through service, not taken as a right. — From our fifth value, Gratitude & Care
