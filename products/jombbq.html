JOMBBQ Charcoal Briquettes

Long-burning sawdust charcoal briquettes for the finest BBQ experience — for backyard grills, satay stalls and restaurant kitchens.

Tap a photo to enlarge

JOMBBQ is a sawdust charcoal briquette made for long, even burning. Compressed into uniform hollow-core sticks, each briquette lights reliably and holds a steady heat — so a grill stays hot through a full evening of satay, ayam panggang or seafood without constant topping-up.

For Sabah's retailers, charcoal is a strong seasonal and festive mover — Hari Raya, Chinese New Year, Kaamatan and every long weekend drive BBQ demand. For foodservice customers it's an everyday working supply.
