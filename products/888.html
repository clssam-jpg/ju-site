888 Tea & Coffee

A Malaysian heritage brand — part of the Thong Guan group since 1942, the year it all began with a bicycle, fifty dollars and a sack of tea leaves.

The 888 brand is the original Thong Guan product — the tea that a young Ang Thong Guan sold from his bicycle in Sungai Lalang, Kedah in 1942. Eight decades later, 888 remains a living heritage brand within the group, sold across Malaysia through the group's distribution channels.

For Jaya Uni'ang, distributing 888 is both a commercial line and a family matter — it's our parent group's foundational product. We carry the full range across our four Sabah warehouses, supplying provision stores, coffee shops, supermarkets, hotels and foodservice operators throughout the state.

Product ranges we distribute

  • Ceylon tea — black tea leaves and tea dust for traditional teh tarik and teh 'O' preparations in kedai kopi and mamak stalls.
  • 888 Coffee — roasted and ground coffee for kopitiam-style brewing: kopi 'O', kopi susu and the full Malaysian coffee-shop repertoire.
  • 3-in-1 sachets — convenience coffee and tea mixes for home and office.
  • Specialty blends and gift packs — festive and premium presentations.

Exact SKUs and pack sizes vary by season and promotion. Contact our team for the current stock list and trade pricing.

What makes 888 distinct isn't marketing — it's the quiet consistency of a brand that has been part of Malaysian coffee-shop culture for more than eight decades. A Ceylon tea that works in teh tarik. A kopi 'O' blend that pulls dark, bitter and honest. These aren't aspirational brand moments; they're the small dependable pleasures of daily Malaysian life.— How we approach the tea & coffee category
