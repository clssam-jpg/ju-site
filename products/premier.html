Premier Tissues

Tissue paper products for homes, hotels, restaurants and offices — one of the most reliable-turn SKUs on any Sabah retail shelf.

Tissue paper is a quiet hero of the FMCG aisle — a product almost nobody thinks about until it's missing, and one that every household, office and foodservice outlet buys regularly. Premier is our tissue partner, stocked across the full Sabah network to match the category's fundamental characteristic: constant demand.

The category has evolved well beyond basic facial tissue into kitchen towels, bathroom tissue, dinner napkins and hospitality-specific formats. We carry the breadth of the Premier range so our retail and HoReCa customers can offer their own customers a full choice.

Product ranges we distribute

  • Facial tissue — pocket pack, box and multipack formats.
  • Kitchen towels — for home and commercial kitchen use.
  • Bathroom tissue — single and multi-roll packs.
  • Hospitality tissue formats — for hotel, restaurant and catering.

Exact SKUs and pack sizes vary by season and promotion. Contact our team for the current stock list and trade pricing.

In the hospitality channel especially, tissue paper is a silent service indicator. A hotel room with thoughtfully stocked tissue. A restaurant with the right napkin at the table. These are small touches that matter to the end customer, and our job is making sure the supply chain behind them never falters.— How we approach the tissues category
