EpalJac Biscuits

Biscuits that serve the daily teatime ritual — stocked in provision shops, coffee shops and petrol stations across Sabah.

EpalJac is part of our biscuit range — a category sitting somewhere between staple and indulgence in the Malaysian shopping basket. Teatime, lunchbox, kids' snack, office pantry, kedai kopi companion — biscuits are one of the most versatile product types on the FMCG shelf.

For Jaya Uni'ang, EpalJac is distributed across our four warehouses to supply the full retail spectrum — from supermarkets and mini-marts down to the smallest kedai runcit in the rural interior.

Product ranges we distribute

  • Cream wafers — EpalJac Wafer in lemon and chocolate flavours.
  • Cream-filled biscuits — the popular sweet-filled format.
  • Plain and savoury biscuits — for teatime pairing.
  • Family-size packs — for household consumption.
  • Individual and multipack formats — for lunchbox and convenience channels.

Exact SKUs and pack sizes vary by season and promotion. Contact our team for the current stock list and trade pricing.

Biscuits are a category where breadth of assortment matters. A shopkeeper wants two or three brands, several varieties each, at a reasonable shelf turn. Our job is to help them build that assortment — both through principal brands like EpalJac and through complementary products that round out the category.— How we approach the biscuits category
