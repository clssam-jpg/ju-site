Brand · Baby Diapers

Diapex

Baby diapers for Sabah's parents — reliable, available and priced for daily family budgets. One of the most trust-sensitive categories we carry.

Diapers are a deeply personal purchase. No parent experiments casually — once a family finds a brand that fits, feels right and suits the household budget, they stick with it. Diapex is our baby diaper partner, built for parents who want dependable performance at a price that works for daily family life.

Because diaper purchases are high-frequency and continuous — from newborn through toddler, across two to three years per child — availability is everything. Our four-warehouse model means Diapex stocking in Sandakan and Tawau is as reliable as it is in Kota Kinabalu.

Product ranges we distribute

  • Newborn sizes — for the first weeks and months.
  • Infant and toddler sizes — through the main stages of growth.
  • Pants-style formats — for active toddlers.
  • Bulk and economy packs — for monthly family shopping.

Exact SKUs and pack sizes vary by season and promotion. Contact our team for the current stock list and trade pricing.

The baby care aisle is one of the most loyalty-driven sections of any retail store. Parents who find a brand they trust want it in stock, week after week. That means the distributor-retailer relationship has to be built on consistency, not price-cutting — because what the parent values most is knowing the brand will be there when they need it.— How we approach the baby diapers category
