D'Jack Snacks

Snack foods loved across Malaysian supermarkets, provision stores and convenience outlets — the small indulgence that fills the basket at every checkout.

D'Jack is part of our snack food portfolio — a brand serving the impulse-purchase, convenience-snack and casual-entertaining segments of the FMCG market. Across Sabah's retail shelves, snack food is a high-velocity category, and distribution has to keep pace.

Our D'Jack distribution serves provision stores, modern trade (supermarkets and mini-marts), convenience channels including petrol-station c-stores, and the general trade. Weekly routes keep the SKUs moving, and trade promotions keep the category fresh.

Product ranges we distribute

  • Savoury snacks — the bulk of the category.
  • Family and share pack sizes — for casual entertaining and household consumption.
  • Single-serve formats — for the convenience and petrol-station channel.
  • Promotional and seasonal SKUs — tied to festive occasions and trade calendars.

Exact SKUs and pack sizes vary by season and promotion. Contact our team for the current stock list and trade pricing.

Snack foods live or die at the point of purchase — the shelf space, the placement, the colour of the pack catching the customer's eye as they queue at the till. Our job isn't just to deliver cases; it's to make sure the product presents itself the way the brand owner intended.— How we approach the snack food category
