Our story · since 1983

A Sabah original, grown with the state we call home

Founded in January 1983 in Kota Kinabalu, Jaya Uni'ang began as a local plastic packaging distributor. Over four decades we have grown into a full-fledged FMCG trading house — offering sales, marketing, brand development and logistics services across all of Sabah.

  • Locally rooted. Four warehouses, staffed by Sabahans who know their regions.
  • Nationally backed. A wholly owned subsidiary of Thong Guan Industries Bhd (KLSE: TGUAN).
  • Manufacturing depth. Sister company Uniang Plastic Industries makes packaging in Inanam, Kota Kinabalu.

Who we serve

From village shops to five-star hotels

Our growing customer base spans every corner of Sabah's consumer economy. Whatever channel you operate in, we have a route and a team ready for you.

Provision stores & supermarkets

The backbone of Sabah's retail — from the kampung shop with hand-written prices to the major chain with planogram discipline. Weekly restocking, trade-friendly pricing and promotional POS that arrives on time.

Hotels & resorts

From beachside resorts in Kota Kinabalu and Sandakan to business hotels statewide — F&B, amenities and back-of-house consumables on a steady rota.

Restaurants & cafés

Consistent supply for every kind of operator, from kedai kopi to specialty cafés. Cooking oil, beverages, snacks and packaging, delivered to schedule.

Petrol stations

Convenience-store SKUs for forecourt retail across all major networks — quick-turn beverages, snacks and tissues. Always in stock, always on time.

Bakeries

Ingredients, packaging and dry goods for both artisan bakeries and high-volume operators. Reliable case supply that doesn't stall production.

Hardware stores

Plastic packaging, household consumables and utility products — the side of the catalog that keeps Sabah's hardware shops stocked beyond just tools.
