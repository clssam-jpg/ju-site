Sports Day 2026

A day of badminton and futsal that ended with smiles, snacks and well-earned trophies, and a celebration of the team spirit behind everything we do.

3 photos · tap any photo to enlarge

The whole Jaya Uni'ang family came together for Sports Day 2026, a day of friendly competition on the badminton court and the futsal pitch.

The day closed with smiles, snacks and well-earned trophies, and with special congratulations to our cash prize winners. A massive shoutout to our event organisers and to everyone who took part.

Events like these remind us that our greatest strength is our unity and shared team spirit.— Thank you to the entire Jaya Uni'ang family. Until the next game!
