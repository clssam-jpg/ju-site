For brand owners

Put your brand on every shelf in Sabah

East Malaysia is not a smaller version of the Peninsula. It has its own geography, its own trade structure and its own consumer habits — and it rewards a distributor who has spent four decades learning them.

If you're a principal weighing up how to enter or grow in Sabah, the questions are usually the same: who will actually stock my product, how fast can it get there, and how will I know what's happening on the ground?

This page answers those three questions directly.

Who will stock your product

Our trade teams work eight channels — provision stores and supermarkets, hotels and resorts, restaurants and cafés, petrol stations, bakeries, hardware stores, wholesalers and institutional buyers. Each channel has named account executives who know the buyers personally, in many cases across two decades of trading.

How fast it gets there

Four warehouses — Kota Kinabalu, Tawau, Sandakan and Lahad Datu — put every major population centre in Sabah within a single working day's drive of stock. All four carry the full catalog, so a launch reaches rural and urban outlets in the same wave rather than trickling outward from the capital.

How you'll know what's happening

Sell-through by outlet, stock cover, ageing, returns and shelf-share — reported monthly, without you having to ask. Local teams add the context the numbers don't carry: why a SKU is moving in Tawau but not Sandakan, which competitor just cut price, what the trade is asking for next.

How a partnership starts

Four steps, roughly ninety days

01

Conversation

Tell us your category, pack formats, target channels and current East Malaysia position. We'll be candid about fit.

02

Coverage plan

We come back with a route plan, outlet-count estimate by channel, pricing structure and indicative service levels.

03

Launch

Stock lands in all four warehouses, trade teams are briefed, POS is prepared, and the first sell-in wave runs statewide.

04

Review

Monthly reporting from month one, with a formal category review each half-year to reset targets and assortment.

Why principals choose us

What a four-decade distributor brings

Genuine statewide reach

Not “Kota Kinabalu plus occasional east-coast runs”. Four warehouses, all carrying full range, all running weekly routes.

A listed-company parent

Thong Guan Industries (KLSE: TGUAN) brings governance, transparency and long-term investment horizons to the relationship.

Local teams, long tenure

Sabahans who know the roads, the seasons and the shopkeepers. Many of our staff have been here ten, twenty, thirty years.

Category, not just logistics

We take a view on assortment, pricing and promotion — and we'll tell you when we think a plan won't work in this market.

Manufacturing next door

Sister company Uniang Plastic Industries makes packaging in Inanam, which shortens lead times on packaging-linked requirements.

We say no sometimes

If we can't do a brand justice in this market, we'll say so early rather than take the listing and under-serve it.
