Plastic Packaging

Where our story began in 1983. Industrial-grade packaging manufactured locally by our sister company, Uniang Plastic Industries in Inanam — and distributed statewide through Jaya Uni'ang.

Jaya Uni'ang started life as a plastic packaging distributor. Four decades later, packaging is still core to our business — not because we haven't evolved, but because the category sits at the centre of everything Sabah's economy does.

Our packaging products come from two sources: our sister company Uniang Plastic Industries (Sabah) Sdn. Bhd. — which manufactures multilayer laminated barrier films, general packaging and shopping bags from its plant in Inanam, Kota Kinabalu — and our parent group Thong Guan Industries Bhd, the largest stretch film manufacturer in Malaysia.

The range

What we carry

01

HDPE Bags

High-density polyethylene bags — shopping bags, produce bags, industrial bags. Available in various sizes and gauges.

02

LDPE Bags

Low-density polyethylene bags for food, retail and industrial use. Softer, more flexible feel than HDPE.

PP Bags

Polypropylene bags for bakery, confectionery and foodservice — clear, stiff, ideal for product display.

Stretch Films

Pallet wrapping film for warehousing, logistics and manufacturing — conventional and premium nano-layer specifications.

Food Wrap (PVC)

Cling wrap for food preservation, produce packaging and hospitality use — made by Thong Guan, one of Malaysia's largest PVC food wrap producers.

06

Specialty Packaging

Custom-printed bags, laminated barrier films and specific-use packaging — available on request through Uniang Plastic Industries.

Who buys our packaging products

Unlike our F&B and household lines which go primarily to retail, our packaging customers span a wider spectrum:

  • Hardware stores — reselling HDPE, LDPE and PP bags and stretch film to end users.
  • Bakeries and food processors — PP bags, food wrap, laminated packaging.
  • Warehousing and logistics firms — stretch film for pallet wrapping.
  • Manufacturers — industrial bags, specialty films, custom-printed packaging.
  • Agricultural and farm operations — heavy-duty industrial bags.
  • Laundries and hospitality — general packaging and bag supplies.

Why our packaging operation is different

Manufactured locally

Because our sister company makes products right here in Kota Kinabalu, we avoid long-distance shipping lead times and can respond quickly to custom requirements.

Backed by a listed-company parent

Thong Guan Industries (KLSE: TGUAN) holds ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certifications, and operates the first 33-layer nano-technology film line in Asia Pacific. That's the manufacturing depth sitting behind every roll of stretch film we deliver in Sabah.

Sustainability-conscious

Our parent group was the first in Asia Pacific to commercialise bio-based polyethylene stretch film, in partnership with Dow Chemical. As the conversation around plastic sustainability evolves, we're positioned to evolve with it.

Packaging is where we started, and packaging is still where we set our highest standards — because it's where the group's manufacturing expertise is deepest. — On four decades in the packaging trade
