Join us

Build a career with a Sabah original

We've been growing since 1983 — and we grow because Sabahans want to work, stay and advance with us. Whether you're an experienced FMCG professional or just starting out, we'd like to hear from you.

Distribution work takes different shapes — the warehouse team packing orders before dawn, the drivers covering interior routes, the sales executives meeting retail customers, the accounts team reconciling month-end, the branch managers running each warehouse as its own small business. A functioning FMCG distributor needs all of them.

What we offer isn't flashy. It's stable, meaningful work with a company that has been part of Sabah's economy for four decades, backed by a publicly listed parent, with genuine career paths across four warehouses and every function.

What it's like

Working at Jaya Uni'ang

A company that's been around

We opened in 1983, and many colleagues have been with us ten, twenty, even thirty years. That's not a recruiting slogan — it's the actual tenure profile of our staff.

A real career path

We'd rather promote from within than hire from outside. Our branch managers mostly came up through sales or operations; our senior accounts staff mostly started as clerks.

Cross-warehouse exposure

Four warehouses in different parts of Sabah means geographic mobility for staff who want it. A sales executive in KK can request a transfer to Tawau.

Group opportunities

We're part of the Thong Guan group, which operates manufacturing, trading, R&D and distribution businesses across Malaysia and internationally.

What we look for

  • Reliability. Our whole business is built on customers being able to count on us — which means we need people we can count on.
  • Willingness to learn. FMCG distribution has a lot of small details. Nobody arrives knowing them all; we expect people to pick them up.
  • Respect for customers and colleagues. We work in a relationship business, and how you treat the shopkeeper on your route matters as much as how you treat the CEO.
  • Pride in work done well. A properly packed pallet, a correctly reconciled ledger, a delivered-on-time order — we notice these things.
If you want a career that rewards showing up, getting better and earning your way — we'd like to meet you. — On hiring at Jaya Uni'ang
