Who we serve

From village shops to five-star hotels

Every month our trucks reach thousands of customers across Sabah — from a single-shopfront kedai runcit in a coastal village to the loading bay of a major resort.

Distribution isn't an abstraction at Jaya Uni'ang. It's the weekly rhythm of vans pulling into shop forecourts, the standing order at a hotel back-of-house, the rebalancing call between two warehouses when a SKU runs short on the east coast.

The channels below describe how we organise our trade teams — but behind every channel is a relationship, often built over years. We segment this way because each channel buys differently, restocks at a different cadence, and values different aspects of what we do.

Trade channels

Eight ways our stock reaches Sabah

Provision stores & supermarkets

The backbone of Sabah's retail — from the kampung shop with hand-written prices to the major chain with planogram discipline. Weekly restocking, trade-friendly pricing and promotional POS that arrives on time.

Hotels & resorts

From beachside resorts in Kota Kinabalu and Sandakan to business hotels statewide — F&B, amenities and back-of-house consumables on a steady rota.

Restaurants & cafés

Consistent supply for every kind of operator, from kedai kopi to specialty cafés. Cooking oil, beverages, snacks and packaging, delivered to schedule.

Petrol stations

Convenience-store SKUs for forecourt retail across all major networks — quick-turn beverages, snacks and tissues. Always in stock, always on time.

Bakeries

Ingredients, packaging and dry goods for both artisan bakeries and high-volume operators. Reliable case supply that doesn't stall production.

Hardware stores

Plastic packaging, household consumables and utility products — the side of the catalog that keeps Sabah's hardware shops stocked beyond just tools.

Wholesalers

Bulk pricing, scheduled deliveries and flexible credit terms. The trade-to-trade backbone that keeps secondary distribution flowing into Sabah's interior.

And many more

Schools, pharmacies, caterers, mini-marts, plantation canteens and institutional buyers — wherever fast-moving consumer goods need to land in Sabah.
