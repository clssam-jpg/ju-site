Our story

A Sabah original since January 1983

Four decades of moving what Sabah consumes — from a small plastic packaging distributor in Kota Kinabalu to the state's full-fledged FMCG trading house.

Jaya Uni'ang Sdn. Bhd. was founded on 15 January 1983 in Kota Kinabalu, Sabah. What began as a small, locally-owned plastic packaging distributor has, across four decades, grown into one of East Malaysia's most established fast-moving consumer goods distributors — a trading house that delivers value to everyday life.

We didn't start with brand names on our vehicles. We started with a conviction: that Sabah's retailers, hotels, bakeries and provision shops deserved the same reliable supply chain and dedicated service that Peninsular Malaysia took for granted. That conviction has guided every expansion, every warehouse opening, and every partnership we've formed since.

From packaging to full-range FMCG distribution

In the early years we focused on what we knew best: plastic packaging products serving Sabah's industrial, food and consumer sectors. Word travelled. Retailers who trusted us with their packaging asked whether we could also stock their shelves with everyday essentials. They asked because reliability is rare — and over time we built a full catalog around their needs.

Today Jaya Uni'ang provides market coverage for a wide range of fast-moving consumer products: Food & Beverage (F&N, 888 tea & coffee, Alif cooking oil, D'Jack snack food, EpalJac biscuits and more), Household & Personal Care (CleanMom detergents, Premier tissues, Diapex baby diapers and others), and the plastic packaging range that remains our heritage.

Rooted locally. Backed nationally.

Jaya Uni'ang is a wholly owned subsidiary of Thong Guan Industries Bhd — a company listed on Bursa Malaysia (KLSE: TGUAN) and the largest stretch film manufacturer in Malaysia. Our sister company, Uniang Plastic Industries, manufactures packaging locally from its plant in Inanam, Kota Kinabalu.

Being part of the Thong Guan group means our customers benefit from three things at once: the local responsiveness of a Sabah-based distributor, the supply reliability of a listed-company parent, and the manufacturing depth of a sister company that makes the product in the same state.

One partner, three advantages

Local Sabah roots. National manufacturing backbone. One trusted partner for the full FMCG range.

What we do today

From our head office in Kota Kinabalu and warehouses in Tawau, Sandakan and Lahad Datu, we serve a growing list of customers including provision stores, supermarkets, wholesalers, hotels and restaurants, petrol stations, bakeries and hardware stores — across every major population centre in Sabah.

Our services extend beyond basic distribution. We offer sales, marketing, brand development and logistics — partnering with brand owners to grow their presence in the East Malaysian market, and partnering with retailers to keep their shelves stocked with products their customers want.

A trading house that delivers value to everyday life. — Our promise, lived out every delivery day since 1983

The path ahead

Our vision is to be “Your Preferred FMCG Partner in ASEAN and Beyond” — a phrase we take literally. With four decades of Sabah expertise, the backing of a regional parent company, and a distribution network that touches every major port in the state, we're well positioned to help brand owners reach new markets, and to help retailers serve a state that is growing, diversifying and demanding more every year.

Whether you're a multinational brand seeking a distribution partner, a retailer looking for a reliable supply network, or a Sabahan looking to build a career in the FMCG industry — we'd love to hear from you.

Milestones

Four decades, four branches

1983Founded

Jaya Uni'ang opens in Kota Kinabalu

Founded on 15 January as a local plastic packaging distributor on Mile 5, Jalan Penampang — still our head office today.

1984Expansion

Tawau warehouse opens

Just one year in, the south-east coast gets its own base — a gateway to Sabah's agriculture and palm-oil hinterland.

1987Expansion

Sandakan warehouse opens

Coverage extends to the historic east-coast trading port and its interior reaches along the Kinabatangan.

1989Expansion

Lahad Datu warehouse opens

East-coast coverage completed, serving the town and the surrounding palm-oil and agricultural belt.

1997Group

Thong Guan Industries lists on the KLSE
