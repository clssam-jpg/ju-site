Our values

Five principles. Four decades. One constant.

Values aren't slogans on a wall. They're the filter for every hiring decision, every partnership, every invoice, every delivery. These are ours.

No. 01

Customer First

Every decision starts with what serves the customer best. We earn the relationship every delivery, every week, every year. Customer-first isn't a strapline for us — it's the bias we apply when there's a hard choice between short-term margin and long-term relationship. The long-term relationship wins, almost always.

What this looks like

Flexible credit terms for customers going through a tough season. A phone call to confirm stock before a supermarket's promo week. A driver who knows the shopkeeper by name. A willingness to hold inventory for a customer when we could move it faster elsewhere.

Value 01 of 05

A filter for hiring, partnerships, invoicing and delivery — not a slogan on a wall.

No. 02

Teamwork

We win together — across our four warehouses, across functions, across the generations of Sabahans who built this company. No single warehouse, department or individual succeeds alone in distribution. A sale only counts when the driver delivers, the accounts team invoices correctly, the warehouse picks accurately, and the customer is satisfied.

What this looks like

Cross-warehouse support when one branch runs short and another has stock. Senior staff mentoring new hires regardless of office. Account executives riding along on delivery routes to see the customer's actual experience. Problems solved as “our problem”, not “their department's problem”.

Value 02 of 05

A filter for hiring, partnerships, invoicing and delivery — not a slogan on a wall.

No. 03

Innovate

Continuously evolving how we distribute and deliver value. From paper ledgers in 1983 to modern supply chains today — a forty-year-old business stays relevant by changing how it operates while keeping why it exists the same. Innovation doesn't only mean technology; it means rethinking route planning, warehouse layouts, credit assessment and customer communication when a better way shows up.

What this looks like

Adopting modern inventory systems across all four warehouses. Experimenting with route optimisation software. Being open to new categories — e-commerce packaging, speciality F&B — when the Sabah market calls for them. Listening to junior staff when they see a better way; they usually do.

Value 03 of 05

A filter for hiring, partnerships, invoicing and delivery — not a slogan on a wall.

No. 04

Integrity

We keep our word. Every invoice, every shipment, every time. It's why customers stay with us for decades, not months. Integrity in distribution means refusing shortcuts that would hurt the customer: selling stock we don't have, mis-stating delivery dates, hiding product issues, or inflating invoices. It's not heroic — it's just the baseline.

What this looks like

Telling a customer “we can't deliver on Tuesday, but we can on Wednesday” rather than “sure, Tuesday”. Proactively flagging an invoice error before they notice. Refusing to carry counterfeit or dubious-origin products regardless of margin. Paying suppliers on agreed terms — every time.

Value 04 of 05

A filter for hiring, partnerships, invoicing and delivery — not a slogan on a wall.

No. 05

Gratitude & Care

Thankful to our partners who grew with us. Caring for our people, customers and the Sabahan community we're part of. A distributor in a state as diverse as Sabah — with its many communities, its distances, its weather, its ecosystems — doesn't operate in isolation. We owe something back.

What this looks like

Long-term supplier relationships that outlast individual executives on either side. Employee welfare programmes, training investment and internal promotions. Supporting Sabahan causes quietly, without publicity. Remembering that the privilege to operate in a state is earned through service, not taken as a right.

Value 05 of 05

A filter for hiring, partnerships, invoicing and delivery — not a slogan on a wall.
